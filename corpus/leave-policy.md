# Time Off and Leave Policy

## Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 1.67 vacation days per month of service, which is 20 days per year. From the fifth anniversary of their start date, full-time employees accrue 25 days per year. Part-time employees accrue vacation in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year, and carried-over days must be used by March 31. Days above that are lost unless your director approved an exception before December 1.

## Requesting time off

Request time off in the Harbor HR portal. An absence of 3 days or more must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines the request in Harbor.

## Sick days

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. A note from a doctor is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave

Employees with at least 6 months of service may take parental leave with a salary top-up to 80 percent of their regular salary for up to 18 weeks, in addition to any government benefits.

## Bereavement leave

Employees receive 5 paid days of bereavement leave for an immediate family member and 2 paid days for an extended family member.
