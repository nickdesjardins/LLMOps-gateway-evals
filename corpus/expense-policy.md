# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Scope and submission

This policy applies to every employee and to contractors who hold a Cedar Ridge Naturals corporate card. Submit each expense report in the Ledger portal within 30 days of the date of the expense. Reports submitted more than 90 days after the expense are not reimbursed unless the Chief Financial Officer approves an exception in writing.

## Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for every expense of 25 dollars or more. A credit card slip alone is not a receipt. If a receipt is lost, attach a missing receipt declaration signed by your manager; an employee may file at most two missing receipt declarations per calendar quarter.

## Meals and entertainment

The daily meal limit while travelling is 75 dollars for domestic travel and 110 dollars for international travel, per person, tips included. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of client entertainment approved in advance by a director. Client entertainment is capped at 150 dollars per attendee, and the report must list every attendee and the business purpose.

## Mileage and ground transport

Use of a personal vehicle for business is reimbursed at 0.68 dollars per kilometre; commuting between home and your usual office is not reimbursable. Taxis and ride sharing are allowed. Car rentals must be booked through the travel desk and must be mid-size or smaller.

## Approval thresholds

An expense report up to 2,500 dollars is approved by your direct manager. A report from 2,500.01 to 10,000 dollars is approved by your department director. A report above 10,000 dollars requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer. Nobody may approve their own expenses, and an approver who reports to the claimant must send the report one level up.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: personal entertainment, traffic fines and parking tickets, seat or room upgrades not allowed by the travel policy, gifts to government officials, annual fees of personal credit cards, and hotel minibar charges.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved expense reports are paid in the next biweekly payroll run. Corporate card statements must be reconciled in the Ledger portal by the fifth business day of each month.
