# IT Service Desk Guide

## Contacting the service desk

Open a ticket in the service desk portal or call extension 4357. The service desk is staffed from 7:00 to 19:00 Eastern time on weekdays. The P1 line is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for critical outages and security incidents.

## Ticket priorities

P1 is a critical outage that affects many users, or a security incident: first response within 15 minutes and a resolution target of 4 hours. P2 is a major function degraded for a team: first response within 1 hour and resolution within 1 business day. P3 is a problem for a single user who has a workaround: first response within 1 business day. P4 is a request or a question: first response within 3 business days.

## Approved hardware

The standard laptop is a 14-inch business laptop from the approved hardware catalogue, replaced every 4 years. Designers and developers may request a higher-performance model with their director's approval. Personal computers may not be connected to the office network.

## Software requests

Request software through the service desk portal; only titles from the approved software catalogue are installed. A licence that costs more than 500 dollars a year needs your director's approval. Installing software that is not in the catalogue is not allowed.

## VPN and remote access

The VPN is required to reach internal systems from outside the office, and it uses multi-factor authentication. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN connected.

## Password resets

Reset your own password in the self-service portal. When the service desk resets a password for you, it verifies your identity with your employee ID and your manager's name. The service desk will never ask for your current password.
