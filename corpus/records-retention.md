# Records Retention Schedule

## Purpose

This schedule says how long each kind of record is kept and how it is destroyed afterwards. When a record fits more than one category, the longest retention period applies.

## Financial records

Invoices, receipts, tax records and bank statements are kept for 7 years after the end of the fiscal year they belong to.

## Batch and quality records

Batch production records, certificates of analysis and quality deviation reports are kept until one year after the expiry date of the batch they describe, and never for less than three years.

## Employee records

Personnel files are kept for 7 years after the employee leaves the company. Files of unsuccessful job candidates are kept for 1 year after the position is filled.

## Email

Email is kept for 3 years and then deleted automatically, unless it is under a legal hold or has been filed as one of the records above.

## Legal hold

A legal hold issued by the legal department overrides this schedule: records under hold must not be deleted or altered until legal releases the hold. Each person who receives a hold notice must acknowledge it within 2 business days.

## Disposal

At the end of its retention period, paper is shredded through the secure shredding service and electronic records are deleted with a certified wipe performed by IT.
