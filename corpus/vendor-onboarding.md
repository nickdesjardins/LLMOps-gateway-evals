# Supplier Onboarding Procedure

## When this procedure applies

Every new supplier must complete onboarding before the first purchase order is issued. Procurement owns the procedure and gives the final approval.

## Required documents

A new supplier provides a W-9 form (United States) or its business tax registration number (Canada), a certificate of insurance showing at least 2 million dollars of general liability coverage, and the signed supplier code of conduct.

## Ingredient suppliers

Suppliers of ingredients also complete the quality questionnaire, provide a certificate of analysis for every lot, and pass a quality audit, on site or remote, before their first lot is accepted. Quality assurance approves ingredient suppliers before procurement does.

## Banking details and verification

Accounts payable enters a supplier's bank details only after calling the supplier back on a phone number already on file, never a number given in the same email or letter. A request to change bank details follows the same callback, needs the approval of two people in accounts payable, and the first payment to the new account is held for 5 business days.

## Payment terms

The standard payment terms are net 45 days. An early payment discount such as 2 percent for payment within 10 days is accepted when a supplier offers it.

## Approval and competitive quotes

Procurement approves every new supplier. When the expected spend with a supplier is above 50,000 dollars per year, procurement must obtain at least two competitive quotes before approval.
