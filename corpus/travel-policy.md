# Business Travel Policy

## Booking

All business travel is booked through the Wayfinder travel portal. Flights must be booked at least 14 days before departure; a later booking needs a short justification approved by your manager in Wayfinder. Personal travel combined with a business trip is allowed when it adds no cost to the company.

## Air travel class

Book economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy is allowed when the scheduled flight time is six hours or more. Business class requires approval from a vice president and is considered only for flights longer than ten hours.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is 225 dollars in tier 1 cities (Toronto, Vancouver, New York, Boston and San Francisco) and 175 dollars everywhere else, before taxes. Book the corporate rate whenever Wayfinder offers one.

## Domestic travel approval

A domestic trip requires your manager's approval in Wayfinder before anything is booked. Domestic means travel within the country where your home office is located.

## International travel

An international trip requires approval from a vice president at least 21 days before departure. Before leaving, the traveller must complete the travel security briefing in the learning portal and register the trip with the company's travel assistance provider. Every laptop taken abroad must be encrypted as the information security policy requires, and travellers to destinations on the security team's high-risk list must take a loaner laptop instead of their own.

## Per diem and incidentals

Incidental expenses such as laundry and tips for hotel staff are reimbursed up to 15 dollars per day. Meals follow the limits of the expense reimbursement policy.

## Changes and cancellations

Cancel any booking you will not use within 24 hours of knowing it. Unused airline credits belong to the company and are tracked in Wayfinder for the next trip.
