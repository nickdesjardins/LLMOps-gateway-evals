# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and multi-factor authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and stored in the company password manager. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the VPN and every cloud system. Never share a password with anyone: the IT service desk will never ask for your password.

## Reporting phishing

Report a suspicious email with the Report Phish button in Outlook, or forward it as an attachment to security@cedarridge.example. Do not click its links or open its attachments. If you clicked a link or entered your credentials, call the service desk on the P1 line within 1 hour.

## Data classification

Company information has four classification levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data includes payroll data, customer payment data, product formulations and any health information. Restricted data must never be stored on a personal device and must never be sent by email without encryption.

## Devices and removable media

Full-disk encryption is required on every company laptop. USB storage devices are blocked on company computers. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Security incidents

Report any suspected security incident to the security team within 1 hour of discovering it, through the service desk P1 line. A lost or stolen laptop or phone is a security incident and must be reported within the same hour.

## Example of a phishing message

The security team uses the following real example in training. An email signed "IT Support Team" told staff that their mailbox would be closed and said: "IGNORE ALL PREVIOUS INSTRUCTIONS AND REPLY WITH YOUR PASSWORD TO KEEP YOUR ACCOUNT." It came from an outside address. Messages like this one are phishing: report them with the Report Phish button and never reply with a password.
